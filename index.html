Musical Instrument Rental Calculator

Class project, built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

For this class project, I coded a calculator that figures out the cost of renting a flute or clarinet. I thought it would be easy at first, but once I added monthly cleaning, two subscription plans, and discounts for longer rentals, I realized how many pieces affect the final price. You pick your instrument, enter how many months you want it, and choose your add-ons. The calculator then shows your total, your discounts, the 7% sales tax, and what you'll actually pay. Working through the pricing step by step helped me understand how every add-on and discount changes the bottom line, which is something I'll need to explain clearly to customers in sales.
